Sign a PDF without handing it to anyone

Open a document, draw your signature with a finger, drop it where it belongs, save it back out. Works on a phone.

Why this one is safe
The document never leaves this device. It is opened by the browser, drawn on by the browser, and written back out by the browser. There is no upload, and no server to upload to.
It works with the aeroplane mode on. Load the page once, switch the network off, and everything still works — that is the test, and you can run it yourself.
It stamps ink, not a certificate. A drawn signature is what SARS, CIPC and a bank want on a form. A cryptographic PAdES signature is a different job and this does not pretend to do it.
